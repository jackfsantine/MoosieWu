About Inspired By Love

Inspired By Love is a local Flint, Michigan brand creating modern apparel for men and women. We focus on couple sets that feel connected, plus individual pieces that stand alone with confidence.

Our logo’s lightbulb symbolizes ideas, connection, and the spark that shapes each collection.

What We Sell

  • Shirts for everyday essentials and statement graphics
  • Hoodies in matching couple sets and solo fits
  • Sweatpants built for comfort and coordinated styling
  • Hats that complete the look
