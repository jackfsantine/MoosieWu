About Inspired By Love

Inspired By Love is a Flint, Michigan clothing company focused on matching pieces for couples. Our mission is simple: premium comfort, clean design, and styles that celebrate connection.

From cozy hoodies to coordinated sets, each product is designed so men and women can wear looks that feel personal and unified.

Our Promise

  • Simple, sleek apparel design.
  • Inclusive sizing for both men and women.
  • A Shopify-ready shopping experience.

Flint Roots

Founded in Flint, we serve customers nationwide through our online store while staying connected to our local community.
